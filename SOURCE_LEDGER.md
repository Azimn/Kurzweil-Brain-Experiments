# Source Ledger

This ledger separates facts used to build the developmental environment from adult material used to justify the evaluation phenotype. That separation is part of the experimental firewall.

## Developmental-environment sources

Lemelson-MIT, Raymond Kurzweil inventor profile: https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/raymond-kurzweil

The profile reports an academic and artistic family, a musician/conductor father, a visual-artist mother, encouragement of creativity, building projects in early childhood, computer access through an uncle who was a Bell Labs engineer, early programming, high-school pattern-recognition work, correspondence with Marvin Minsky, MIT attendance, study across computing and creative writing, and an early college-matching business.

Academy of Achievement, Ray Kurzweil interview and biography: https://achievement.org/achiever/ray-kurzwell/

The interview provides additional first-person developmental context, including a supportive intellectual household, intense project orientation, early fascination with computers, teachers who listened seriously to his ideas, Minsky treating him seriously while he was still a high-school student, MIT as a supportive environment, and the observation of a practical college-choice information problem.

## Adult-phenotype sources

Academy of Achievement, Ray Kurzweil interview and biography: https://achievement.org/achiever/ray-kurzwell/

Adult themes used only for the answer key include persistence, selecting tractable problems, using criticism to improve thinking, treating technological trends quantitatively, practical application of technical ideas, long-horizon reasoning, and communicating unconventional technical claims.

Lemelson-MIT inventor profile: https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/raymond-kurzweil

The profile documents repeated cross-domain technical invention, pattern-recognition systems, technologies for accessibility, music technology, entrepreneurship, and sustained work across artificial intelligence and human-computer interaction.

MIT News, Lemelson-MIT prize profile: https://news.mit.edu/2001/lemelson-0425

This source documents the reading machine, the role of a chance encounter with a blind traveler in identifying a practical application for existing OCR technology, and Kurzweil's broader pattern of combining technical capabilities with human problems.

Kurzweil, How My Predictions Are Faring: https://www.kurzweilai.net/images/How-My-Predictions-Are-Faring.pdf

This source documents the use of quantitative historical technology trends and exponential trajectories as a basis for long-range forecasting.

## Important limitation

Public biographies disproportionately preserve unusual achievements and retrospective explanations. They are not complete records of childhood or causation. The developmental timeline in this project is therefore an experimental reconstruction, not a claim that these events caused the real adult personality.
